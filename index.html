The Hub
Tucked along Jassie Street in Poblacion, Santa Maria — the road going to Boracay — South Coast Pickleball Leisure Hub turned a former banana chips warehouse into the region's liveliest court. Palms sway courtside, the surface runs purple-to-lilac under the lights, and open play runs every night from 6 PM to midnight. Energetic, tropical, and welcoming: beach-leisure meets serious play.
